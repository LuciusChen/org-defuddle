We laid out our position in an earlier essay [fn:1], and have held it consistently.

1. The first concern is that capable systems are hard to monitor once released [fn:2].
1. The second concern is that testing should apply to every sufficiently capable system [fn:3].

These positions are complementary rather than competing.

[fn:1] See Sections 2 and 3 of that essay for the full discussion.

[fn:2] See [[https://example.com/report][this report]] for supporting analysis.

[fn:3] Testing regimes are described in /Appendix B/ of the same document.

Practical experience grows through deliberate work on meaningful problems. Choose a small project, understand the constraints, and explain the decisions you make along the way.

** Keep practicing

Ask for feedback, investigate unexpected results, and write down what you learn. Repeating this process helps turn individual lessons into reliable engineering judgment.
